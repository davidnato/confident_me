# User guide

ConfidentMe helps you build confidence in social situations through small,
optional steps. Its idea is simple: **confidence grows through practice, not
perfection.**

> ConfidentMe is a self-help and confidence-building tool. It is not a
> substitute for professional mental health care, and it does not diagnose,
> treat, or cure anything. If anxiety is getting in the way of your life, or you
> are struggling, please talk to a doctor or a mental health professional. If
> you are ever in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

You are always in charge. Every challenge can be skipped, postponed, made
easier, or repeated. Nothing is ever required.

## Getting started

### Welcome screen

When you first open the app you see a short explanation of how it works, a
privacy note, and two choices:

- **Get started** takes you through a few optional questions.
- **Continue as guest** skips them and goes straight to the app. You can answer
  the questions later.

No account is needed either way.

### The optional questions

There are five short steps. Every one can be left blank, and **Skip setup** (top
right) saves a blank profile and takes you to the app.

1. **What should we call you?** A name or nickname, if you like.
2. **Which situations would you like to feel more confident in?** Choose any
   categories, or none.
3. **What would you most like to improve?** Choose one, or none.
4. **How challenging do social situations feel right now?** A slider from 1 to
   5. This is not a score. It only picks a gentle starting difficulty: if you
   choose 3 or higher you start with Beginner challenges, and if you choose 1 or
   2 you start with Intermediate.
5. **What is one small goal you would like to achieve?** Keep it small and kind.

Your answers are used only to choose which challenge to suggest. You can change
them any time from **Home > Change my answers**.

## Home

Home shows, from top to bottom:

- A greeting (and a settings icon in the top right).
- A gentle reminder card, only if you turned reminders on (see Settings).
- **Today's small step**: one suggested challenge with a **Try one small step**
  button.
- **Today's affirmation**. Tap it to open the affirmations screen.
- **Your progress**: your level, streak (if shown), challenges completed,
  categories explored, and your most recent completions.
- Quick links to **Breathing**, **Practice** and **Journal**.
- **Your focus**: what you told us during setup, with a button to change it.

### How today's challenge is chosen

The suggestion comes from fixed rules, not from artificial intelligence. It
prefers challenges in the categories you chose, near the difficulty you prefer,
that you have not completed yet. It leaves out challenges you postponed (until
the day you chose) and ones you skipped in the last three days, and if you just
skipped something it suggests something easier. If you started a challenge, it
suggests finishing that one. The same day gives the same suggestion.

## Challenges

The **Challenges** tab is a library of 28 small challenges in eight categories:
starting conversations, everyday interactions, expressing opinions,
assertiveness, public speaking, social participation, self-confidence, and
handling awkward moments. Use the category and difficulty chips to filter.

Each challenge shows how long it takes, what to prepare, step-by-step
instructions, a reflection question, and the XP it is worth, all **before** you
begin. It also reminds you that you never have to approach strangers, share
private details, or do anything that feels unsafe.

On a challenge you can:

- **Start this challenge**, then **Mark as completed** when you have done it. You
  are offered an optional place to write a reflection.
- **Make this easier**, which shows a smaller version of the same step you can
  choose instead.
- **Skip for now** or **Postpone** (it comes back tomorrow). Neither costs you
  anything.
- **Do it again** after completing it. A repeat is a new attempt.

## Breathing

Open **Practice > Breathing exercise** (or **Breathing** on Home).

- A circle grows as you breathe in, stays full while you hold, and shrinks as you
  breathe out, with the phase and a countdown in the middle.
- The default pattern is **4 seconds in, 4 hold, 6 out**. Pick **No hold 4-6** or
  **Gentle 3-4** to skip holding your breath, or set your own timings with the
  plus and minus buttons (set the hold to 0 to skip it).
- Choose a session length of 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes.
- **Start**, **Pause**, **Resume** and **Stop** work at any time. Stopping early is
  fine and simply ends the session.
- Finishing a full session earns 5 XP and shows a short, kind message.

If you feel dizzy or uncomfortable, stop and return to normal breathing.
Breathing exercises can help some people feel calmer, but they do not work the
same way for everyone and they are not a treatment. If your phone has "reduce
motion" turned on, the circle stays still and the words guide you instead.

## Conversation practice

Open **Practice** and choose one of nine situations: introducing yourself,
joining a conversation, keeping a conversation going, asking follow-up
questions, giving and receiving compliments, politely disagreeing, setting
boundaries, speaking in a meeting, and handling an awkward pause.

Each has ways to start, follow-up questions, and active listening tips. **Rehearse
this** walks you through a few moments: you read what the other person says, type
or say your reply, and can tap an example reply for ideas.

- Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.
- You can leave at any time. Only a finished rehearsal counts, and it earns 5 XP.
- The examples are not predictions. Real people respond in all kinds of ways.

## Affirmations

Open **Practice > Affirmations**.

- There are 24 encouraging, realistic affirmations in four themes: staying calm,
  self-acceptance, progress and connection.
- Tap the heart to save favorites. Use **Favorites only** to see just those.
- Choose themes to shape your daily affirmation and the list. Choose none to see
  everything.
- **Write your own** (up to 160 characters). Your own affirmations can be
  deleted at any time, and they can be part of your daily affirmation.

## Journal

The **Journal** tab is a private place to reflect. Each entry has six optional
parts: what happened, what you felt, what you feared might happen, what actually
happened, what you learned, and what you would like to try next time. A few
optional prompts are one tap away if you want a nudge.

- Entries **save automatically as you type**. A new entry is only kept once it has
  some text.
- Tap an entry to edit it. Delete from the list or the editor (it asks first).
- Entries are stored only on your device. See the privacy section below.

## XP, levels, streaks and achievements

Taking a step earns XP, a gentle record of your effort. It is not a score and it
is never compared with anyone.

| What you do | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Complete a Beginner challenge | 10 |
| Complete an Intermediate challenge | 20 |
| Complete an Advanced challenge | 30 |
| Complete a personal milestone | 50 |
| Finish a breathing session or a conversation rehearsal | 5 |

- Each completed attempt earns its XP **once**.
- **XP only ever goes up.** Skipping a challenge or missing a day never removes
  anything.
- **Levels:** reaching the next level takes 100 × your current level in XP. Level 1
  to 2 takes 100 XP, level 2 to 3 takes another 200, level 3 to 4 another 300,
  and so on. Your level is always worked out from your lifetime XP.
- **Streak:** the number of days in a row you did something (a challenge,
  practice, a milestone or a journal entry). A streak never affects XP, and one
  from yesterday still counts until you have had your chance today.
- **Achievements** celebrate effort and consistency, for example completing your
  first challenge, trying three categories, finishing a breathing session, or
  writing a first journal entry. Locked ones show how to earn them.
- Level-ups and new achievements appear in a small, quiet dialog (no flashing).
- Don't want any of it? Turn off **Show XP, levels and streaks** in Settings (or
  at the top of the Progress tab). Your progress is still saved quietly, and
  turning it back on shows everything.

## Progress

The **Progress** tab shows your level and streak (if shown), your counts of
challenges, practice sessions and journal entries, a **This week** calendar of the
last seven days, which of the eight categories you have explored, your personal
milestones, your achievements (with the most recent first), and your XP history.

### Personal milestones

Write your own milestones, such as "Started a conversation", "Shared an
opinion", "Attended an event", "Asked for help" or "Spoke in a meeting" (the
suggestions are one tap). Mark one done when you have done it to earn 50 XP.
Deleting a milestone never takes away XP you already earned.

## Settings

Open Settings from the gear icon on Home or Progress.

- **Appearance:** choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**, and one of five
  colors (Lavender, Ocean, Mint, Rose, Sunrise). Both are saved.
- **Challenges:** your preferred difficulty (used for the daily suggestion), and a
  switch to turn the daily suggestion off. With it off, Home shows a button to
  browse the library instead.
- **Reminders:** optional and off by default. If you turn it on and pick a time,
  a card on Home asks, "Would you like to try one small confidence-building
  activity today?" after that time, only if you have not done anything yet that
  day. **Not today** hides it until tomorrow. Reminders appear inside the app
  only; the app does not send phone notifications. They never use guilt.
- **Show XP, levels and streaks:** as described above.
- **Privacy:** a plain-language summary of what is stored and where.
- **Your data:**
  - **Copy my data** puts a complete copy of your data (including your journal)
    on the clipboard as text. It asks first.
  - **Reset progress** clears your challenge history, XP, levels, achievements,
    milestones and practice history, and keeps your journal, your own
    affirmations, your settings and your profile.
  - **Delete all my data** erases everything the app stores and returns you to the
    welcome screen. You must type DELETE to confirm.
- **About:** the app version and the self-help notice.

## Privacy in one paragraph

Everything stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics and no
tracking, and the app does not send your information anywhere. The app does not
add its own encryption; your data is protected by your phone's lock screen and
the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes
everything. See `docs/DATA_AND_PRIVACY.md` for the details.
